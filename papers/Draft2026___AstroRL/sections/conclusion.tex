\section{Conclusion}
AstroRL offers a functional account of astrocytic memory in navigation: persistent calcium dynamics retain recent spatial experience, while predictions of action outcomes make that memory available to action selection through inhibitory regulation. The comparison with neuronal traces supports the hypothesis that astrocytic dynamics could fulfill a similar memory function, without requiring a calcium-specific performance advantage. This interpretation suggests a biological test: whether disrupting persistent astrocytic signaling weakens the influence of recent spatial experience on subsequent choices.
